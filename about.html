How I work

  • Definitions before screens. On identity and access I agreed what each concept is called and how it relates before drawing anything, so screens could change without the model changing.
  • Evidence over opinion, including my own. On Cloud I started sure everything should be unified. A study of AWS, Azure and GCP proved me wrong, and I changed course.
  • Outcomes over output. The campaign platform moved once someone paid for it. I design for the business case as well as the user.
  • Humans approve, AI executes. I use AI tools to prototype and build faster, and I design AI features so a person sets the goal and signs off the result.
