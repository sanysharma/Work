About

Hands-on where it's hardest

I take on the most ambiguous, high-stakes problems personally and turn them into platforms that enterprise customers and leadership can trust.

At Airtel Digital, I own design for the Cloud platform end to end, from its August 2025 launch to Identity & Access Management, and lead a team of two across CPaaS/IQ, IoT and Martech, including Airtel's first AI-powered campaign features.

Before Airtel, I designed lending and KYC journeys at HDFC Bank and commerce flows at Tata CLiQ. I started in communication design at NIFT Mumbai and later studied HCI at IDC, IIT Bombay.
