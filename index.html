About

Hands-on designer, platform thinker.

I'm a Senior Lead Product Designer at Airtel. I lead design for UCLM, Airtel's unified campaign platform, and its first AI features, and I'm designing identity and access management for Airtel Cloud, whose design I led through its launch in August 2025.

I'm hands-on in the work and lead two designers across four product lines: messaging (CPaaS), marketing tech, IoT and Cloud. Much of my influence is with people I don't manage: product managers, engineers, vendors and business teams who build to a design because the evidence and the prototype make the case.

How I work

  1. Definitions before screens. On identity and access I agreed what each concept is called and how it relates before drawing anything, so screens could change without the model changing.
  2. Evidence over opinion, including my own. On Cloud I started sure everything should be unified. A study of AWS, Azure and GCP proved me wrong, and I changed course.
  3. Outcomes over output. The campaign platform moved once someone paid for it. I design for the business case as well as the user.
  4. Humans approve, AI executes. I use AI tools to prototype and build faster, and I design AI features so a person sets the goal and signs off the result.

My team's work

  • Campaigns. A designer on my team owned UCLM Phase 2 design end to end: Flow Builder, maker-checker approvals, dynamic roles, new channels and dashboards.
  • Business name display. Designed by a designer on my team: ₹4.6 crore in its first three months, 8,253 active customers.
  • Cloud. Enterprise clients grew from 18 at launch to 35 by October 2026.
  • IoT. We support the platform's design requests day to day and are working on a UX overhaul.
